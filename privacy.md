# Privacy Policy for Zevorix AI

**Effective Date / Last Updated:** November 2026

Zevorix AI ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect
* **User Inputs:** Texts, queries, or voice commands that you intentionally input while interacting with the AI assistant.
* **Permissions Used:**
  * **Microphone:** Used exclusively for voice commands and speech recognition when interacting with the assistant (no audio is recorded or stored in the background without your knowledge).
  * **Internet:** Used to communicate with AI model APIs to process your inputs and generate responses.
  * **Storage & File Access:** Allows you to select and read local files or code snippets for review and editing upon your explicit request.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
* **Service Provision:** To operate the AI assistant features, generate accurate responses, and efficiently execute your requested tasks.
* **Local Memory & Preferences:** Facts and project notes are stored locally on your device to enhance user experience and maintain conversation continuity, without sharing them with malicious third parties.

### 3. Data Security & AI API Keys
* API keys for AI models (such as Gemini and others) are stored securely and encrypted locally on your device.
* We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to any external parties.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the app constitutes your agreement to these terms.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via the project repository or support channels.
